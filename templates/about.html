WHO WE ARE

Managing Fly Ash Transportation Simply & Efficiently

The Fly Ash Trading & Transport Management System is designed to manage the complete transportation process of fly ash in an organized way.

The system connects the Plant, Transporter and Customer and helps manage orders, vehicles, transportation and payment records.
